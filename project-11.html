Service App

Service App is a tablet-oriented mobile solution for field technicians working at water treatment stations. The application improves execution speed of service tasks, provides on-site context, and synchronizes operational data with central systems.

Back to Projects

Project Overview

The project was designed to support technicians in daily maintenance activities, inspections, and incident reporting. A strong focus was placed on clear mobile UX, offline resilience, and rapid action workflows.

  • Field Service Workflow

    Implemented service tickets, checklists, and status transitions tailored to on-site operations.

  • Alarm & Event Monitoring

    Added alarm visibility and event history for faster diagnostics and better response planning.

  • Mobile-First Interface

    Designed interaction patterns for tablets with focus on readability and quick navigation.

  • Backend Synchronization

    Connected mobile modules to REST APIs for secure data exchange and near real-time updates.

Unique Project Features:

  • Technician-Centered UX:

    Optimized for glove-friendly interaction, clear states, and low cognitive load.

  • Data Consistency:

    Structured synchronization model ensures reliable information between field and office teams.

  • Scalable Architecture:

    Prepared to extend functionality for additional object types and workflows.

Project Outcomes:

  • Improved service cycle transparency and technician productivity.
  • Faster incident registration and better quality of operational data.
  • Higher consistency between field reports and central service planning.
