Corporate Website (W-Team)

A corporate website built for a transport company to present services, strengthen brand credibility, and improve B2B communication. The project focused on clear information architecture, fast loading, and conversion-oriented content structure.

Back to Projects

Project Overview

The website was created to help W-Team communicate its offer in a professional and accessible way. The implementation included page structure planning, visual direction, and CMS-based content management optimized for ongoing business updates.

  • Corporate Information Architecture

    Structured pages around services, company profile, and trust-building proof points.

  • Custom UI/UX Layer

    Designed a clean visual style with strong typography, hierarchy, and consistent branding.

  • CMS Implementation

    Built with WordPress and Elementor for flexible content management by non-technical teams.

  • SEO & Performance

    Implemented SEO-ready structure and optimization for faster rendering and discoverability.

Unique Project Features:

  • B2B Communication Focus:

    Messaging and layout tailored for transport clients and business partners.

  • Conversion-Oriented Sections:

    Clear call-to-action placement to improve contact and inquiry rates.

  • Maintainable CMS Setup:

    Content blocks organized for easy updates as services evolve.

Project Outcomes:

  • Improved professional brand presentation in online channels.
  • Better clarity of services for potential B2B clients.
  • Provided a scalable website foundation for future growth and campaigns.
